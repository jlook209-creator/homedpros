How It Works
This is how Home D Pros is designed to work. The app that does it is being built now.
01
Post the job
Say what you need done and where. Any trade on the list, any zip code.
02
See who is local
Browse the pros listed for that trade in your area, the way you once would in the phone book.
03
We help connect you
Home D Pros passes your job to matching local pros and introduces the ones who want it.
04
You decide
You and the pro agree on the work and the price between you. You are never obliged to hire anyone.
Straight Labels
Every Listing Says What It Is
A directory is only useful if you can tell what you are looking at. Each pro will carry one of three plain labels.
📒
Public Listing
A local business listed from information it already publishes. It has not signed up with us, and we say nothing about its work.
🙋
Claimed
The pro has confirmed the listing is theirs and filled in their own details. Shown as what the pro tells us.
📄
Licence Checked
We have looked the pro's licence up with their state board and show the number, so you can look it up too.

Home D Pros connects homeowners with independent local pros. Home D Pros is not a contractor and does not perform home-improvement work. We show no ratings or reviews until real customers have left them.
